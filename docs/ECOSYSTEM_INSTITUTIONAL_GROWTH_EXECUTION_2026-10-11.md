## P0 — experience and existing gates
Preserve field validation and provider authority gates. Redesign PersonalTrip around map, rich destination cards, a prominent next action, actual ticket/booking state, visit journal and clear replan. Validate real device screenshots, tap targets, scroll and deep links before any claim of usability.

## Transaction evidence path
Provider admission -> raw snapshot + checksum -> freshness and availability signal -> user choice -> confirmed replacement itinerary -> explicit acceptance receipt -> durable visit/booking history. A proposal is not a booking; pending, cancelled, failed and fulfilled states must never be conflated.

## Differentiated extension lanes
P1: Personal travel memory: confirmed visits, private notes, optional photos with retention/export/deletion and provenance, accessible offline view.
P1: Service disruptions: venue closure, delayed entry, transport changes -> impact explanation -> verified alternatives -> user-approved reroute -> recalculated plan; never fabricate travel times, reservations or supplier consent.
P1: Spatial heritage narrative linked to archivally attributed sources and uncertainty, piloted only after anchor accuracy gate.
P2: Tourism partner kit: venue onboarding, approved provider API contracts, verified availability timestamps, content/rights registry, opt-in referrals and transparent settlement audit.
P2: Institutional city dashboards based on consented aggregated data, no covert visitor tracking or unverified claims of footfall.

## Ownership and proof
One source authority per venue, booking, personal itinerary and evidence artifact. Project-style responsibility lanes merely reference existing owner/action records. Gates: exact-head CI, real provider dual-source proof, offline recovery, device matrix, evidence receipts and measured successful visit completion.
