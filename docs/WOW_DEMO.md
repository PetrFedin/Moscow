# Moscow — Romanov WOW demo

## Purpose

A short presentation path for stakeholders that demonstrates the product concept without replacing the resident/tourist navigation with a presentation interface.

The demo is enabled in web preview, development builds, or when `EXPO_PUBLIC_DEMO_MODE=1`.

## Two-minute flow

1. Open `WOW DEMO`.
2. Show the 1857 Public Domain archival source.
3. Move the archive opacity control to explain the transition from archive evidence to the current facade.
4. Continue to the 3D time machine.
5. Switch `1857` ↔ `1859 / 1883`.
6. Switch trust mode:
   - `Только подтверждённое` — documented geometry only;
   - `+ реконструкция` — documented + reconstructed; hypothesis is physically absent from the public GLB.
7. Rotate/inspect the model in preview or align it with the real facade in the native AR build.
8. Open an evidence hotspot and play the linked audio story.
9. Show the source provenance attached to the hotspot.
10. In a field build, open calibration and show the 5 / 10 / 15 m residual-measurement protocol.
11. Enter the portal to demonstrate how the same spatial content can continue into an immersive scene / Quest build.

## Claims allowed during the demo

- two source-backed historical spatial states are bundled in the app;
- the application distinguishes documented geometry from research reconstruction;
- hypothesis geometry is excluded from the normal public GLB variants;
- the same spatial content pipeline is prepared for mobile AR and VR;
- manual calibration and a measurable field-verification protocol are implemented;
- the 1857 image used in the archive lens is identified as Public Domain by Wikimedia Commons.

## Claims not allowed yet

Do not claim that:

- the current Romanov model is museum-grade or dimensionally final;
- the facade alignment is field-verified;
- a persistent visual/cloud anchor has been selected or validated;
- MapKit is production-configured before the project API key is supplied;
- the portal interior is a historically validated reconstruction;
- the Quest experience has passed device testing.

## Current external gates

1. Yandex MapKit project API key.
2. Measured envelope and exact facade control-point coordinates.
3. On-site test on Varvarka across several supported iOS/Android devices.
4. Historical expert acceptance of public-visible reconstructed geometry.
5. Quest/Meta setup and device validation for the VR branch.
